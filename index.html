Restaurants & cafés

Pakkhús

By the harbour in a 1932 house. Famous for local langoustine, lamb & duck. Book ahead in summer.

Kaffi Hornið

Central log-house restaurant since 1999. Seafood, lamb, pizza, veggie. kaffihornid.is

Ósinn

Restaurant on the ground floor of Hótel Höfn. Fresh local produce & fish.

Íshúsið Pizzeria

Thin stone-baked pizzas by the harbour, in the old icehouse.

Hafnarbúðin

Cheerful old-school diner — burgers, fast food with a local twist. Late hours.

Nettó deli

Ready meals, bakery & hot food if you want something quick and cheap.

Opening hours are seasonal — most are open for dinner in summer, but call ahead for large groups.

On a day off — sights nearby

Stokksnes & Vestrahorn

Dramatic black-sand beach & mountain, ~15 min. Iconic photo spot; small entry fee at the Viking Café.

Jökulsárlón & Diamond Beach

Glacier lagoon with floating icebergs, ~1 hr west. Boat tours in summer.

Vatnajökull glacier

Guided glacier hikes & ice-cave tours from the region — book with an operator.

Gamlabúð

Vatnajökull National Park visitor centre at the harbour — maps, exhibits, info.

Ósland

Coastal nature spot by town — great for a walk & birdlife (arctic terns, puffins in season).

Þórbergssetur

Culture museum ~30 min west at Hali — plus a café/restaurant.

Good to know

Getting around
Höfn is small & walkable. Djúpivogur (raft days) is ~1 hr north; Reykjavík ~460 km / 6 hrs west.
Weather
Changes fast, even in summer. Bring layers, waterproofs & sturdy shoes. Check vedur.is and road.is.
Money
Cards work everywhere — you rarely need cash.
Hours & numbers were correct at time of writing (July 2026) but can change — when in doubt, call ahead or ask at Nettó / Gamlabúð.
