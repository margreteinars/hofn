Good to know on set

Call times
Week 1 working time 06:30–18:30 · Week 2 (Djúpivogur) 14:00–02:00. Watch for the daily call sheet.
Weather
Changes fast even in summer — bring layers, waterproofs & sturdy shoes. Check vedur.is & road.is.
Getting around
Höfn is small & walkable. Reykjavík is ~460 km / 6 h west — plan drives with daylight & weather in mind.
Hours & numbers were correct at time of writing (July 2026) but can change — call ahead or ask at Nettó / Gamlabúð if unsure.
